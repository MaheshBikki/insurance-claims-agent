POLICY DOCUMENT: STANDARD AUTO INSURANCE
Policy Type: Auto
Applies to: POL1001, POL1004

1. COVERAGE SUMMARY
This policy provides coverage for accidental damage, collision, theft, and third-party
liability arising from the use of the insured vehicle. Coverage limit and deductible
are specified individually on each policyholder's schedule.

2. WHAT IS COVERED
- Collision damage: repair costs resulting from a collision with another vehicle or object.
- Theft: total loss or partial loss due to theft of the insured vehicle or its parts.
- Fire and natural calamity: damage caused by fire, flood, storm, or earthquake.
- Third-party liability: bodily injury or property damage caused to a third party.

3. WHAT IS NOT COVERED (EXCLUSIONS)
- Claims filed within the first 7 days of policy issuance for theft are subject to
  additional verification and may be denied if sufficient documentation is not provided,
  as this window is associated with higher fraud risk.
- Damage caused while driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
- Damage caused by using the vehicle for commercial purposes unless a commercial
  rider is separately purchased.
- Wear and tear, mechanical breakdown, or manufacturing defects.
- Consequential or indirect losses (e.g., loss of income due to vehicle unavailability).
- Any claim submitted after the policy end date or during a lapsed/expired period.

4. CLAIM SETTLEMENT
Approved claims are settled up to the coverage limit stated on the policyholder's
schedule, minus the applicable deductible. Claims exceeding the coverage limit will
be settled only up to that limit; the policyholder is responsible for any excess.

5. FRAUD AND MISREPRESENTATION
Any claim found to involve misrepresentation of facts, inflated claim amounts, or
incidents inconsistent with the policy's coverage terms may be denied and referred
for further investigation.
