POLICY DOCUMENT: STANDARD PROPERTY INSURANCE
Policy Type: Property
Applies to: POL1003

1. COVERAGE SUMMARY
This policy provides coverage for physical damage to the insured property caused by
fire, natural calamity, theft, or other insured perils, up to the coverage limit
stated on the policyholder's schedule.

2. WHAT IS COVERED
- Fire damage: destruction or damage to the structure and contents caused by fire.
- Natural calamities: damage caused by flood, storm, cyclone, or earthquake.
- Burglary and theft: loss of contents due to forced entry.
- Damage caused by falling trees, aircraft, or vehicles.

3. WHAT IS NOT COVERED (EXCLUSIONS)
- Any claim submitted after the policy end date. Coverage strictly ends on the
  policy's stated end date; claims for incidents occurring after expiry, or claims
  filed after expiry regardless of when the incident occurred, are not payable
  under this policy.
- Gradual damage such as dampness, rot, or pest infestation.
- Damage arising from the policyholder's own negligence in maintaining the property
  (e.g., failure to repair a known structural defect).
- Loss of cash, jewelry, or valuables beyond the sub-limit specified on the schedule.
- War, riot, or nuclear-related damage.

4. CLAIM SETTLEMENT
Approved claims are settled up to the coverage limit stated on the policyholder's
schedule, minus the applicable deductible, based on the assessed repair or
replacement cost of the damaged property.

5. FRAUD AND MISREPRESENTATION
Claims involving inflated damage estimates, staged incidents, or misrepresentation
of the property's condition at the time of policy purchase may be denied and
referred for further investigation.
