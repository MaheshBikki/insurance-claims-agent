POLICY DOCUMENT: STANDARD HEALTH INSURANCE
Policy Type: Health
Applies to: POL1002, POL1005

1. COVERAGE SUMMARY
This policy provides coverage for hospitalization, surgical procedures, and related
medical treatment for the insured. Coverage limit and deductible are specified
individually on each policyholder's schedule.

2. WHAT IS COVERED
- In-patient hospitalization expenses (room charges, doctor's fees, nursing charges).
- Surgical procedures, including pre- and post-operative care within 15 days of the
  procedure.
- Diagnostic tests directly related to a covered hospitalization.
- Ambulance charges up to a reasonable limit for emergency transport to a hospital.

3. WHAT IS NOT COVERED (EXCLUSIONS)
- Any pre-existing condition diagnosed before the policy start date, for the first
  24 months of continuous coverage.
- Routine health checkups, wellness visits, or preventive care not tied to an
  active medical condition requiring hospitalization.
- Cosmetic or elective procedures not medically necessary.
- Treatment received outside a network hospital without prior authorization,
  unless it is a genuine medical emergency.
- Dental treatment and vision care, unless resulting directly from an accident
  covered under this policy.
- Any claim submitted after the policy end date or during a lapsed/expired period.

4. CLAIM SETTLEMENT
Approved claims are settled up to the coverage limit stated on the policyholder's
schedule, minus the applicable deductible. For cashless treatment at a network
hospital, the insurer settles directly with the hospital; for reimbursement claims,
the policyholder is repaid after review of submitted medical bills and reports.

5. FRAUD AND MISREPRESENTATION
Claims involving inflated hospital bills, treatment inconsistent with the diagnosis,
or misrepresentation of medical history at the time of policy purchase may be denied
and referred for further investigation.
